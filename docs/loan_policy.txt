LOANDESK FINANCIAL - PERSONAL LOAN POLICY (Version 3.2)

1. ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must be UAE residents aged 21 to 60 years. Minimum monthly salary requirement is AED 8,000 for salaried applicants and AED 15,000 average monthly income for self-employed applicants. The applicant must have completed at least 6 months with their current employer.

2. LOAN LIMITS
The minimum loan amount is AED 10,000. The maximum loan amount is AED 500,000 or 20 times the monthly salary, whichever is lower. Loans above AED 200,000 require additional approval from the Credit Committee.

3. INCOME VERIFICATION
For loans above AED 50,000, applicants must provide income proof. Accepted documents are: last 3 months salary certificates, last 6 months bank statements showing salary credits, or an official salary transfer letter from the employer. Self-employed applicants must provide audited financials for the last 2 years and a valid trade license.

4. INTEREST RATES
Interest rates range from 5.5 percent to 12 percent per annum reducing balance, based on the applicant's credit score and salary transfer status. Salary transfer customers receive a 1 percent discount on the standard rate.

5. DISBURSAL RULES
Loan disbursal happens within 48 hours of final approval, directly to the applicant's registered bank account. Disbursals above AED 100,000 require manual approval by a senior loan officer before release. No disbursal is processed on UAE public holidays.

6. EARLY SETTLEMENT
Customers may settle their loan early. An early settlement fee of 1 percent of the outstanding amount applies, capped at AED 10,000.

7. DEFAULTS
A payment is considered late after 5 calendar days past the due date. Three consecutive missed payments classify the loan as delinquent and trigger the collections process.
